# M8's demo ships without `zbx rm` and with one push retry

*Decided 2026-11-11 by the supervisor, at the re-review of #90 (M8 slice 8, the demo); the owner can overrule.*

## No `zbx rm` in M8

The demo needs to drop containers that have ended. M8's brief does not ask for a removal command, and building one would grow the slice past its demo. The demo therefore drops ended containers by hand (`drop` in `accept_m8.zig`). **Cut** from M8; it belongs to a later milestone that owns the container lifecycle.

## `demo-m8` retries the first push once

In the test VM, the first `zbx push` to the local registry sometimes stalls on a blob PUT for about 30 s and ends in E0503. The cause is the VM's port forwarding through pasta, not the push: the bytes reach the registry, and the upload's tail is never answered. The error is correct and names the stall.

The demo retries that first push **once**, and only after that exact E0503 (`check_stalled_push`). Its recorded output keeps both attempts. Any other failure, or a second stall, fails the demo.

**Accepted** as a workaround, not a fix. Zigbox/zigbox#91 tracks the real fix. The retry goes when #91 closes.

**What would change this.** Seeing the stall outside the VM, or with a registry on the host network, would make it a bug in the push. Then #91 blocks the M8 release.
