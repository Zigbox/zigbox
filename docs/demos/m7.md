# M7 demo: the build

This document grows slice by slice. Until the `demo-m7` job is green in CI, M7 is built, not
demonstrated.

## Slice 2: keys, one step, the cache

`zbx build` builds the steps of `zbx.build.zon` one at a time, in graph order, each from its
base in `zbx.lock`; a row per step says `hit` or `built`. `src/testing/vm/main_m7.zig` builds
the `compile` step twice (miss, then hit).

### Known limits (accepted)

- **No cache pruning.** Entries in `build/cache` stay until a later slice prunes them.
- **`store verify` and `store usage` do not know the cache**: they neither check nor count its
  entries.
- **/out size is unbounded until import's limits (E0415).** A step may write as much as the disk
  holds; the import then refuses what is over the store's file, path or depth limits.
- A base that `zbx.lock` names but the store lacks is refused (E0804, fix `zbx pull`); pulling it
  by the locked digest is slice 3.
- A SIGKILLed `zbx` leaves its work directory and run record until the next build's sweep and
  the run layer's reconciliation; the sim tests plant such leftovers, the real kill is the VM
  demo's.
- Farm directories made before slice 2 keep old mtimes: see DECISIONS, "Farm format marker".
