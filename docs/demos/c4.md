# C4 demo notes (Zigbox 0.5.2)

`zig build demo-c4` runs seven steps in the test VM, as the unprivileged `demo` user, against
the Alpine rootfs. The brief is [docs/briefs/zb-c4.md](../briefs/zb-c4.md), the decisions are in
[2026-11-14-zigcube-c4-requests.md](../decisions/2026-11-14-zigcube-c4-requests.md).

| Step | Proves |
|------|--------|
| 1 `start.py` | a `--net none` container holds only `lo` |
| 2 `listen.py` | ZB-5: listeners on 127.77.x.y are made live in the container's net namespace, answer inside, are not reachable from the host, and are gone when closed |
| 3 `connect.py` | ZB-5, ZB-8: a held socket connects inside, and still does after the adopting agent is killed and restarted |
| 4 `net.py` | ZB-7: `zbx exec` joins the container's net namespace |
| 5 `hostile.py` | the workload cannot see or signal the supervisor, `kill -1` leaves both alive, `/etc/hosts` is not writable |
| 6 `refuse.py` | E0610 (`--net host`), E0402, E0220: one line, coded, no errno |
| 7 `hosts.py` | ZB-6: `--hosts-file` is read-only at `/etc/hosts`; an in-place rewrite is seen, a rename-over is not; three bad paths are E0612 |

## Scope cuts

- **Run record not bumped for `--hosts-file`.** `zbx start` of a stopped container does not
  remember the flag (decision file, ZB-6). Not demonstrated.
- **No IPv6, UDP or Unix sockets** (Non-goals of the brief).
- **No second kernel.** The demo runs one guest kernel (Ubuntu 24.04); other kernels are
  not exercised.
